## What Is the History of the Angel Oak?

The tree's exact age is unknown. The [City of Charleston](https://www.charleston-sc.gov/153/Angel-Oak/) estimates 300 to 400 years. [The Cultural Landscape Foundation](https://www.tclf.org/angel-oak-park) puts it at 400 to 500 years.

Key moments in its story:

- **1717:** Colonist Abraham Waight acquired the land, which became part of a plantation known as "The Point."
- **1810:** Martha Waight married Justus Angel. The property and tree became known as "Angel," which is where the name comes from, not the angel-wing shape of its limbs.
- **Early 1900s:** During segregation, the tree was open to everyone. Educator and civil rights activist Septima Poinsette Clark brought students to picnic under its branches, according to TCLF.
- **1991:** The City of Charleston acquired the site and opened Angel Oak Park.
- **2013:** After a grassroots campaign, the Lowcountry Land Trust acquired neighboring woodland that had been slated for development.

The tree has long been a gathering place for local communities, including the Gullah Geechee people, and its long limbs rest on the ground in places. Resurrection ferns grow along the branches.

## How Do You Visit Angel Oak Over Thanksgiving Weekend?

Angel Oak Park is at **3688 Angel Oak Road** on Johns Island. A few tips from the city:

- **Hours:** The park is open seven days a week but **closed on holidays**. Aim for the Friday after Thanksgiving or the weekend, and call ahead to confirm.
- **Cost:** Free. Donations help care for the tree.
- **Rules:** No blankets, food, drinks, props, or tripods on or around the tree. Leashed pets are allowed in designated areas, including the picnic area.
- **Photos:** Permits are required for weddings, engagement shoots, and other special events.

Make a day of it. Johns Island's marshes and oak-lined back roads make for an easy scenic drive. Then head back across the bridge to see the [Holiday Festival of Lights on James Island](/posts/holiday-festival-of-lights-james-island-2026/), near our James Island office.
