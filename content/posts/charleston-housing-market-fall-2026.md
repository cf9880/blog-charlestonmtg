**The Charleston SC housing market is steady this fall. In September 2026, the area's median sales price was $425,000, up 0.7% from a year earlier, while closed sales jumped 9.5%. Homes are taking a little longer to sell, which gives buyers more time and more room to negotiate.**

## Key Takeaways

- **Median price:** $425,000 in September 2026, up 0.7% year over year ([Charleston Trident Association of Realtors](https://ctarmls-public.stats.showingtime.com/docs/mmi/x/report)).
- **Sales are up:** 1,538 closed sales, up 9.5%. Pending sales rose 7.9% to 1,400.
- **More time to shop:** 55 days on market, up from 51 a year ago.
- **Room to negotiate:** Sellers got 95.6% of original list price on average.
- **Rates:** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.40% the week of October 15, 2026.

## What Is Happening in the Charleston Housing Market?

The Charleston Trident Association of Realtors (CTAR) tracks sales across the Charleston Trident MLS. Its September 2026 report, current as of October 9, shows a market that's busy but not frantic ([Charleston Business Report](https://chsbusinessreport.com/stories/charleston-home-sales-september-2026)).

| Metric (Sept. 2026) | Value | Change vs. Sept. 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Closed sales | 1,538 | +9.5% |
| Median sales price | $425,000 | +0.7% |
| Pending sales | 1,400 | +7.9% |
| New listings | 2,008 | -0.5% |
| Homes for sale | 5,590 | about flat |
| Months of supply | 3.6 | down from 3.8 |
| Days on market | 55 | up from 51 |

Through the first nine months of 2026, closed sales were up 5.5% to 14,434, and the year-to-date median price was $435,000, up 2.3%.

## How Do Home Prices Compare Across the Charleston Area?

Prices vary a lot by county. Here are September 2026 medians for **single-family detached** homes from CTAR's local market updates:

- **Charleston County:** $715,000 (up 1.6%)
- **Berkeley County:** $430,000 (up 4.9%)
- **Dorchester County:** $392,000 (down 2.0%)

Townhomes and condos can be a lower-cost way in. In Charleston County, the attached-home median was $457,450 in September. Want a closer look by area? See our [Charleston neighborhood price guide](../charleston-neighborhoods-where-to-buy/).

## How Does Charleston Compare With the Nation?

Nationally, the National Association of Realtors reported a median existing-home price of $417,500 in September 2026, up 1.3%, with sales at a 3.99 million annual pace ([Mortgage News Daily](https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/news/10162026-existing-home-sales-nar-inventory-prices-appr)). The Charleston area's median sits just above the national figure, and local sales are growing faster.

## What Do Mortgage Rates Mean for Charleston Buyers?

Rates are higher than a year ago:

- **Freddie Mac (Oct. 15, 2026):** 30-year fixed at **7.40%**, versus 6.27% a year earlier ([Freddie Mac](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)).
- **Mortgage News Daily (Oct. 20, 2026):** 30-year fixed index at **7.40%**, FHA at 7.02%, VA at 7.04%.

With longer days on market, buyers may be able to ask sellers for **closing cost help** or a **rate buydown**. A buydown uses money, often from the seller, to lower your rate for the first years or for the life of the loan.

## What Is There to Do in Charleston This Fall?

House hunting is more fun when you mix in some Lowcountry fall traditions:

- **Coastal Carolina Fair** (Oct. 29 - Nov. 8): The annual fair returns to Exchange Park in Ladson.
- **Harvest Festival** (Nov. 7): Bluegrass, hayrides, and crafts at Johns Island County Park ([Charleston County Parks](https://www.ccprc.com/news/post/harvest-festival-nov-7-at-johns-island-county-park/)).
- **Holiday Festival of Lights** (starting Nov. 13): A drive-through light show at James Island County Park.

**Fun fact:** Charleston was founded in 1670 as Charles Town, named for King Charles II. That makes fall 2026 the city's 356th year.
