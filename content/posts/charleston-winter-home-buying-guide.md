**Yes, buying a home in Charleston in winter can work in your favor. Fewer buyers shop between Thanksgiving and New Year's, and Charleston-area homes took an average of 56 days to sell last December, up from 50 the year before. That extra time usually means more room to negotiate on price, repairs, or closing costs.**

Here's what the numbers say, how today's mortgage rates fit in, and how to enjoy the Lowcountry holidays while you house hunt.

## Key Takeaways

- **Less competition:** Charleston Trident homes averaged **56 days on market** in December 2025, up 12% from 50 days ([South Carolina REALTORS](https://scr.stats.showingtime.com/docs/rds/2025-12/x/report)).
- **Prices held up:** The December 2025 median was **$433,973**, up 4.7% year over year. Don't expect a fire sale.
- **Rates today:** Mortgage News Daily's 30-year fixed index was **7.33%** on Oct. 26, 2026, near its lowest level in more than a month.
- **Negotiate terms:** Motivated winter sellers may help with closing costs or a rate buydown.
- **Start now:** A preapproval before Thanksgiving makes a December closing far more realistic.

## Is Winter a Good Time to Buy a Home in Charleston?

For many buyers, yes. Freddie Mac notes that winter usually brings **fewer buyers** and that holiday-season sellers are often **motivated to negotiate** on price, closing costs, closing date, or items like appliances ([Freddie Mac My Home](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/blog/homebuying/buy-or-sell-in-winter)).

Local data backs that up. Here is how the Charleston Trident MLS looked last December, from the South Carolina REALTORS December 2025 report:

| Charleston Trident | Dec. 2024 | Dec. 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homes, condos and villas sold | 1,399 | 1,494 | +6.8% |
| Median sales price | $414,556 | $433,973 | +4.7% |
| Average days on market | 50 | 56 | +12.0% |

Sales still happened, and prices still rose. But homes sat longer, which gives a prepared buyer leverage.

Nationally, the gap was even clearer. Redfin estimated that U.S. home sellers outnumbered buyers by **45% in December 2025**, the largest gap in its records going back to 2013 ([Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/news/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Buyers-vs-Sellers-Release-Jan-2026.pdf)).

The latest Charleston Trident Association of Realtors numbers (September 2026) show a balanced market:

## How Do Today's Mortgage Rates Affect a Winter Purchase?

Rates are higher than a year ago but have eased this month:

- **Mortgage News Daily (Oct. 26, 2026):** 30-year fixed **7.33%**, FHA **6.96%**, VA **6.98%**, jumbo **7.60%** ([Mortgage News Daily](https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/mortgage-rates/mnd)).
- **Freddie Mac (Oct. 22, 2026):** 30-year fixed **7.32%**, down from 7.40% a week earlier but up from 6.19% a year ago ([Freddie Mac](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)).

Because rates move daily, ask about **locking your rate** once you're under contract. If a seller is willing to help, a **rate buydown** can lower your payment. A buydown is money paid at closing, often by the seller, to reduce your interest rate for the first few years or for the life of the loan.

## What Can Buyers Negotiate During the Holidays?

When a home has been listed for a while, these requests are common:

1. **Seller-paid closing costs** to reduce your cash to close
2. **A temporary or permanent rate buydown**
3. **Repairs or a repair credit** after inspection
4. **A closing date that fits you**, such as early January
5. **Appliances or furnishings** left with the home

Results vary by home and seller. Your real estate agent can tell you what is realistic for a given listing.

## How Do You Close Before the End of the Year?

Holiday calendars are short. To keep a December closing on track:

- **Get preapproved now.** Have pay stubs, W-2s, two months of bank statements, and ID ready.
- **Avoid new credit.** Hold off on holiday financing for furniture or a new car until after closing.
- **Plan around closures.** Lenders, title companies, and county offices close for the holidays.
- **Buying near the water?** Order flood insurance quotes early. Our [Charleston flood zone guide](/posts/charleston-flood-zone-home-buying/) explains what to check.
- **Eyeing downtown?** Older homes may need extra inspections. See our tips on [buying a historic home in Charleston](/posts/buy-historic-home-charleston/).

## What Is There to Do in Charleston This Holiday Season?

House hunting pairs well with a few Lowcountry traditions:

- **Holiday Festival of Lights** (nightly Nov. 13 to Dec. 31, 2026, 5:30 to 10 p.m.): More than 750 light displays, a holiday train ride, and marshmallow roasting at James Island County Park. It has been a local tradition since 1990 ([Charleston County Parks](https://www.ccprc.com/holiday-festival-of-lights/)).
- **City of Charleston Holiday Parade** (Sunday, Dec. 6, 3 p.m.): The city has set the 2026 date. Last year's route ran along Broad and Meeting streets to Marion Square ([Charleston News Today](https://charlestonnewstoday.com/christmas-parade-and-tree-lighting/)).
- **Holiday farmers markets** in Marion Square on Dec. 5, 6, 12, 13 and 19, from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.

**History note:** Marion Square started out as a militia muster and parade ground and was later used by cadets of The Citadel. In 1882 the city officially named it for Revolutionary War hero Gen. Francis "Swamp Fox" Marion ([Charleston County Public Library](https://www.ccpl.org/charleston-time-machine/brief-history-marion-square-part-1)).
