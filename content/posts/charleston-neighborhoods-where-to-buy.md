**Where to buy near Charleston SC depends on your budget and lifestyle. In September 2026, single-family medians ranged from about $392,000 in Dorchester County, home to much of Summerville, to $1,375,000 in Lower Mount Pleasant. West Ashley, James Island, and Berkeley County fall in between. Here's how they compare.**

## Key Takeaways

- **Lower Mount Pleasant:** $1,375,000 single-family median (Sept. 2026)
- **James Island:** $727,500
- **West Ashley:** $539,000
- **Berkeley County:** $430,000
- **Dorchester County (incl. much of Summerville):** $392,000
- Source: [Charleston Trident Association of Realtors](https://ctarmls-public.stats.showingtime.com/docs/mmi/x/report) local market updates, current as of Oct. 9, 2026.

## Is Mount Pleasant Worth the Price?

Mount Pleasant is one of the Lowcountry's most sought-after towns, with highly rated amenities, easy beach access to Sullivan's Island and Isle of Palms, and the dining scene on **Shem Creek**.

- **Lower Mount Pleasant** (closer to the Ravenel Bridge) had a September median of $1,375,000.
- **Upper Mount Pleasant** came in at $995,000.
- Townhomes are an option: the attached-home median was $490,000 in Lower Mount Pleasant and $560,000 in Upper Mount Pleasant.

**Loan fit:** Many single-family homes here exceed the 2026 conforming limit of $832,750, so **jumbo loans** are common.

## What Is It Like to Live on James Island?

James Island sits between downtown and Folly Beach. It's known for a laid-back island feel and **James Island County Park**, home of the **Holiday Festival of Lights**, which starts November 13 this year.

- Single-family median: **$727,500**, up 6.2%
- Townhouse/condo median: **$395,000**
- Homes sold in 38 days on average in September, faster than the region overall

## Why Do Buyers Choose West Ashley?

West Ashley offers a quick drive downtown and more space for the money. It's also where Charleston began. **Charles Towne Landing**, now a state historic site, marks where English settlers founded Charles Town in **1670** before moving to the peninsula.

- Single-family median: **$539,000**
- Townhouse/condo median: **$387,500**
- Inventory rose about 10% from a year ago, giving buyers more choices

**Loan fit:** Many homes here work well with **conventional** or **FHA** loans.

## Is Summerville a Good Place to Buy?

Summerville, much of which sits in Dorchester County, is known as the "Flower Town in the Pines" and proudly calls itself the birthplace of sweet tea. Buyers come for its historic downtown, newer neighborhoods, and lower prices.

- Dorchester County single-family median: **$392,000**
- Townhouse/condo median: **$287,227**
- Sellers received 96.5% of original list price, so there's some room to negotiate

**Loan fit:** Prices here work well for **FHA** and **conventional** buyers, and some outlying areas may be eligible for **USDA** zero-down loans. Check any address on [USDA's eligibility map](https://eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov/eligibility/welcomeAction.do).

## What About Berkeley County?

Berkeley County includes Goose Creek, Moncks Corner, Hanahan, and parts of Summerville and Daniel Island. Sales jumped 19.7% in September.

- Single-family median: **$430,000**, up 4.9%
- Townhouse/condo median: **$317,430**
- Close to **Joint Base Charleston**, making it popular with military families using **VA loans**

Weekend idea: visit **Old Santee Canal Park** or **Cypress Gardens** near Moncks Corner.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most affordable area near Charleston SC?

Among the areas in this guide, Dorchester County, which includes much of Summerville, had the lowest single-family median at $392,000 in September 2026, per CTAR.

### How much do homes cost in Mount Pleasant?

### Can I get a USDA loan near Charleston?

Possibly. USDA loans offer zero-down financing in eligible areas, and some outlying parts of the Charleston region may qualify. Eligibility is set by address on USDA's map.

It depends on price and your situation. Conventional and FHA fit many homes, VA helps eligible military buyers, USDA fits eligible areas, and jumbo loans cover higher prices.
