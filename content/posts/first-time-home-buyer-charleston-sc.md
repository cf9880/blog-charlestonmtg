## Which Loans Work Best for First-Time Buyers in Charleston?

### FHA loans

An **FHA loan** is insured by the Federal Housing Administration and allows 3.5% down with qualifying credit. It is popular with buyers who are still building credit. See our [FHA loan Charleston SC guide](/posts/fha-loan-charleston-sc/) for limits and mortgage insurance details.

### Conventional loans

A **conventional loan** is not government-insured. Some programs allow 3% down, and private mortgage insurance can be removed later once you build enough equity.

### VA loans

Eligible veterans, service members and some surviving spouses can use a **VA loan** with no down payment required by VA, which is a big help near Joint Base Charleston.

### USDA loans

**USDA loans** can offer no-down-payment financing on eligible properties in qualifying areas, which may include some communities on the edges of the metro. Income limits apply.

## Is There Down Payment Assistance in Charleston SC?

Yes, there are options to ask about. Two statewide programs from [SC Housing](https://schousing.sc.gov/homebuyers/programs-homebuyers/palmetto-home-advantage):

- **Palmetto Home Advantage.** Conventional, FHA, VA and USDA options with forgivable down payment assistance of 0%, 3% or 4% of the loan amount and no monthly payment. SC Housing says it requires a minimum 640 credit score, has no sales price limit and has no first-time buyer requirement. An income limit applies; check SC Housing for the current figure.
- **SC Housing Homebuyer Program.** SC Housing's flyer describes $10,000 in forgivable assistance (a 0% second lien forgiven after 15 years), a $450,000 maximum home price, and first-time buyer rules. Charleston County is a "non-targeted" county, while Berkeley and Dorchester Counties are "targeted."

SC Housing programs are offered through its approved lending partners. Coastal Funding also offers down payment assistance options through several of its lender partners. We will look at your income, credit and target price to see which options may fit. Eligibility is never guaranteed.
