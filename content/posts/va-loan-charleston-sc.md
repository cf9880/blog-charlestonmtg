## How Does a VA Loan Work in Charleston?

The U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs guarantees part of each VA loan. That guarantee lets lenders offer:

- **No down payment** for borrowers with full entitlement, with no VA loan limit.
- **No monthly mortgage insurance,** unlike most low-down-payment loans.
- **Limits on closing costs** borrowers can be charged, and seller concessions of up to 4%.
- **Competitive rates.** VA averages often run below conventional averages.

You'll need a Certificate of Eligibility, which your lender can usually pull online, and the home must be your primary residence.

## What Is the VA Funding Fee in 2026?

| Down payment | First use | Later use |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 5% | 2.15% | 3.3% |
| 5% to less than 10% | 1.5% | 1.5% |
| 10% or more | 1.25% | 1.25% |

*Source: [VA.gov funding fee and closing costs](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/).*

The fee can be financed into the loan. You don't pay it if you receive VA disability compensation, are a Purple Heart recipient on active duty, or are an eligible surviving spouse, among other exemptions.

## What Benefits Does South Carolina Offer Veterans?

South Carolina is a strong place to own a home after service:

- **Property tax relief:** veterans rated 100% permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected condition may qualify for a full exemption on their home and up to five acres, through the SC Department of Revenue.
- **Retirement income:** military retirement pay is fully exempt from South Carolina income tax.
- **Support:** the [SC Department of Veterans' Affairs](https://scdva.sc.gov/) helps veterans with benefits, claims, and local resources.

Confirm your eligibility with the state and a tax professional before you count on any exemption.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I buy a home in Charleston with no money down using a VA loan?

Yes. Eligible veterans and service members with full entitlement can buy with no down payment. You'll still have closing costs, which sellers may help cover with up to 4% in concessions.

### What is the VA funding fee in 2026?

It's 2.15% for first use with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% down, and 1.25% with 10% or more down. Veterans receiving VA disability compensation are exempt.

### Do disabled veterans pay property taxes in South Carolina?

Veterans rated 100% permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected condition may qualify for a full property tax exemption on their home and up to five acres. Check with the SC Department of Revenue.

### Is there a VA loan limit in Charleston?

There's no VA loan limit for borrowers with full entitlement. If part of your entitlement is in use, county conforming limits can affect your down payment.

### When is the Charleston Veterans Day parade in 2026?

The Charleston Veterans Day Parade is Sunday, November 8, 2026, from Market Street to Colonial Lake. Check the city's schedule for the start time.
