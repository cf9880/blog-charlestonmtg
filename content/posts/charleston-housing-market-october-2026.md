**The Charleston housing market in October 2026 showed higher prices but fewer sales. The Charleston Trident Association of REALTORS® (CTAR) reported a median sales price of $445,239, up 4.7% from October 2025, while closed sales fell 6.2% to 1,312. Inventory was steady at 3.6 months of supply, a fairly balanced market.**

CTAR's October report, current as of November 9, 2026, gives the first look at how the Lowcountry handled a month when mortgage rates sat above 7%. Here's what the numbers say and what they mean if you plan to buy or refinance before year-end.

## Key Takeaways

- **Median price:** $445,239 in October 2026, up 4.7% year over year (CTAR).
- **Fewer sales:** 1,312 closings, down 6.2%. Pending sales fell 13.1% to 1,283.
- **Supply is steady:** 5,582 homes for sale and 3.6 months of supply.
- **Homes sold a bit faster:** 53 median days on market, versus 54 a year earlier.
- **James Island stayed strong:** single-family median of $770,000, up 3.6%, in about 30 days.
- **Rates are easing:** Freddie Mac's 30-year average fell to 7.18% on November 5, its third straight weekly drop.

Year to date, closed sales are up 4.7% to 15,787, and the median price is up 2.3% to $435,000. So 2026 is still running ahead of 2025, even with a slower October.

## Why Did Sales Slow While Prices Rose?

A few things likely played a role:

- **Higher rates in October.** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.40% on October 8 and 15, its highest of 2026, compared with about 6% early in the year. Higher payments tend to cool contract signings, which shows up in pending sales.
- **Fewer new listings.** New listings fell 11.7%, so buyers had fewer fresh choices.
- **The mix of homes that sold.** The average price rose 11.5%, faster than the median, which suggests more higher-priced homes closed. A median can move because of what sells, not just because every home gained value.

CTAR's Housing Affordability Index fell to 71 from 81 a year earlier, which reflects higher prices and rates. Lower numbers mean less affordability.

## What Do Mortgage Rates Mean for Charleston Buyers Right Now?

Mortgage News Daily's daily index showed these averages on November 9, 2026:

- 30-year fixed: **7.18%**
- 30-year FHA: **6.82%**
- 30-year VA: **6.83%**
- 30-year jumbo: **7.37%**

**Payment example:** On a home at October's $445,239 median with 10% down, the loan would be about $400,700. At 7.18%, principal and interest would be about $2,715 per month. At October's 7.40% peak, it would have been about $2,775, roughly $60 more. Taxes, insurance, flood coverage, and HOA dues are extra. These are illustrations, not quotes.

For loans above the 2026 conforming limit of $832,750, which includes many James Island and Mount Pleasant homes, you'd be looking at jumbo financing.

## What Should Buyers and Homeowners Do Now?

**If you're buying:**

1. **Get fully preapproved.** Sellers still received 95.8% of their original list price on average, so strong offers matter.
2. **Negotiate on terms.** With 3.6 months of supply, ask about closing-cost help or a seller-paid rate buydown.
3. **Price in flood and wind insurance early.** It can change your budget more than the rate does.
4. **Consider the holiday window.** Fewer buyers shop between Thanksgiving and New Year's. Our [Charleston winter home buying guide](/posts/charleston-winter-home-buying-guide/) explains how to use that.

**If you already own:** Rising prices have built equity for many owners. If you want to tap it without giving up a lower first-mortgage rate, a HELOC may be worth comparing. Our [Charleston HELOC guide](/posts/heloc-charleston-sc-homeowners/) walks through the basics.

## Which Loan Options Fit This Market?

- **Conventional:** as little as 3% to 5% down for qualified buyers, up to $832,750.
- **FHA:** 3.5% down with a 580+ score, up to $690,000 for one unit in Charleston, Berkeley, and Dorchester Counties.
- **VA:** often $0 down for eligible veterans and service members, a good fit for Joint Base Charleston families.
- **Jumbo:** for higher-priced homes above the conforming limit.
- **DSCR:** for investors buying rentals, qualified on the property's rental income.
