## How Many Days Are Left on the Calendar?

From mid-November:

- **Thanksgiving, November 26:** about 11 days, shorter than a typical mortgage close.
- **Christmas, December 25:** about 40 days, possible on a 30- to 45-day contract but tight around holiday closures.
- **December 31:** about 46 days, a more comfortable window if third parties stay on schedule.

[Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/learn/how-long-does-it-take-to-close-on-a-house/), citing a 2026 ICE study, reports an average close of about 37 days. That is a national figure, not a Charleston guarantee. A flood-zone property, a condo, or a jumbo loan can take longer if documents lag.

If you are already under contract, ask whether the current date still works once Thanksgiving and Christmas office closures are mapped out. If you have not started, aim for year-end rather than a pre-Thanksgiving move-in.

## What Local Items Often Add Time?

Charleston files have a few familiar pinch points:

- **Flood insurance and elevation certificates.** Start them as soon as the property is identified. Our [flood zone guide](/posts/charleston-flood-zone-home-buying/) explains why this should not wait until the last week.
- **Historic-home inspections and repair negotiations**, which can stretch the option period.
- **Jumbo underwriting** when the loan amount exceeds the 2026 conforming limit of $832,750. See our [jumbo loan guide](/posts/jumbo-loan-charleston-sc/).
- **The three-business-day Closing Disclosure wait.** The [CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/when-do-i-get-a-closing-disclosure-en-179/) requires you to receive the form at least three business days before closing. Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day do not count.

Build a closing date that is not the last open hour before a holiday.
