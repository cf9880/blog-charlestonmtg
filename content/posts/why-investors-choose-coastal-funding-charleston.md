## How Do DSCR and Jumbo Overlap Here?

**DSCR = monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA** (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and association dues). A ratio of 1.0 means rent covers the payment. Typical industry ranges include about 20% to 25% down, a credit score around 620 to 700+, and a DSCR near 1.0 or higher. Those are not Coastal Funding program terms, and they vary by lender.

Because many Charleston prices sit near or above the conforming limit, some investors also need a **jumbo** structure. A jumbo DSCR or a jumbo conventional investment loan has its own down payment, reserve, and credit rules. Our [jumbo loan guide](/posts/jumbo-loan-charleston-sc/) explains the 2026 limit for primary-residence buyers; investor jumbo guidelines are usually tighter.

## What About Short-Term Rentals?

Some DSCR lenders will use short-term rental income. Others want a longer-term lease. Separately, local governments and HOAs set their own rules on if and how a dwelling can be used as a vacation rental. Those rules can change and can differ between the City of Charleston, nearby towns, and unincorporated areas.

Treat legal use as a due-diligence item, just like title and insurance. Do not underwrite your offer on peak-season rent until you know the property can actually be rented that way.

### What if the Charleston purchase price is above the conforming loan limit?
