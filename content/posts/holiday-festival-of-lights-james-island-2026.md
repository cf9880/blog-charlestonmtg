**The Holiday Festival of Lights at James Island County Park runs nightly from November 13 through December 31, 2026, from 5:30 to 10 p.m. Charleston County Parks says the three-mile drive features more than 750 light displays, and tickets are priced per vehicle from $15 to $45 depending on the night and whether you buy online.**

It's one of the Lowcountry's favorite holiday traditions, and it happens just down the road from Coastal Funding's James Island office. Whether you're a longtime local, visiting family for Thanksgiving or thinking about moving to the area, here's what to know for 2026.

## Key Takeaways

- **Dates:** Nightly, November 13 to December 31, 2026, 5:30 to 10 p.m.
- **Where:** James Island County Park, 861 Riverland Drive, Charleston, SC 29412.
- **Tickets:** $15 to $30 online or $25 to $45 at the gate for a standard vehicle, based on green, yellow or red nights.
- **Santa:** Nightly through December 23, plus daytime and inclusive visits on select December dates.
- **New Year's Eve:** Fireworks display on December 31.

## What Is the Holiday Festival of Lights?

Now in its 37th year, the festival is run by Charleston County Parks and Recreation (CCPRC). According to the [official festival page](https://www.ccprc.com/holiday-festival-of-lights/), it features more than 750 illuminated displays and millions of lights along a three-mile driving loop. After the drive, you can park and walk around. CCPRC's October 13, 2026 announcement lists:

- The Festival Express train ride
- A 50-ton sand sculpture
- An old-fashioned carousel and a climbing wall
- Marshmallow roasting over an open fire
- Holiday gift shops and treats like kettle corn and pretzels

## How Much Are Tickets for the Holiday Festival of Lights in 2026?

To spread out crowds, CCPRC labels each night green, yellow or red based on expected attendance. Prices for a standard vehicle (up to 15 people) are:

| Night | Online in advance | At the gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green (lighter crowds) | $15 | $25 |
| Yellow (moderate crowds) | $25 | $35 |
| Red (heavy crowds) | $30 | $45 |

Tickets are date-specific and advance purchases are nonrefundable, per the [CCPRC ticket page](https://www.ccprc.com/holiday-festival-of-lights/tickets/). Large vehicles pay different rates. Gold Pass members get one free standard-vehicle admission per membership period.

## When Can You See Santa?

CCPRC says Santa is at the festival every night from opening night through December 23. There are also:

- **Mingle with Kringle** daytime visits on Saturday, December 5 and 12
- **Inclusive Santa** visits for people with disabilities and their families on December 6 and 13, in a calmer setting

These special visits require booking through CCPRC.

## Tips for Visiting the Festival

- **Pick a green or yellow night** to save money and time.
- **Buy online** to save up to $15 per vehicle.
- **Thanksgiving week is popular** with visiting family, so check the color calendar before you go.
- **Leave time to walk around** after the drive. The train, carousel and marshmallow roasting are part of the fun.
