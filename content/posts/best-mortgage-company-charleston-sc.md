## What makes the best mortgage company in Charleston SC?

There's no official "best" that fits everyone. A lender that's ideal for a $1.3 million Mount Pleasant home may not be the right fit for a first-time buyer in Goose Creek. Judge every mortgage company in Charleston on the same five things:

1. **Loan options:** conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, jumbo, and investment loans.
2. **Total cost:** rate, APR, points, and lender fees on the Loan Estimate.
3. **Lowcountry know-how:** flood zones, elevation certificates, historic homes, and island properties.
4. **Speed and reliability:** closing on time, every time you can.
5. **Service:** a real person who answers, including outside banker's hours.

## How to choose a mortgage lender in Charleston: checklist

1. **Check your credit** and set a payment you're comfortable with, including flood and wind insurance.
2. **Request three quotes the same day** with matching loan amount, down payment, and lock period.
3. **Compare Loan Estimates** line by line ([CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/loan-estimate/)).
4. **Verify licenses** on [NMLS Consumer Access](https://www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org/).
5. **Look up the flood zone** and get insurance quotes early.
6. **Ask your agent** which lenders communicate well and close smoothly.
7. **Get fully preapproved** before you write offers.

### Questions to ask a Charleston mortgage lender

- Which loan fits me best, and what would my payment be?
- What are the rate, APR, and total lender fees?
- Do you handle jumbo loans and homes in flood zones?
- How long is my rate lock, and what if closing is delayed?
- Do you offer down payment assistance options?
- How will you keep me updated, and how do I reach you after hours?

## Charleston home loan options

- **Conventional:** as little as 3% down for qualified first-time buyers; $832,750 conforming limit in 2026 ([FHFA](https://www.fhfa.gov/news/news-release/fhfa-announces-conforming-loan-limit-values-for-2026)).
- **FHA:** 3.5% down with flexible credit guidelines.
- **VA:** $0 down for eligible veterans and service members, including Joint Base Charleston families.
- **USDA:** $0 down in eligible areas, which can include parts of Berkeley, Dorchester, and outer Charleston counties.
- **Jumbo:** for downtown, Mount Pleasant, Daniel Island, Kiawah, and other higher-priced homes.
- **DSCR:** investment-property loans based on rental income.
- **Down payment assistance:** available through several of our lender partners; eligibility varies.

## Refinancing in Charleston: what homeowners should know

Freddie Mac's 30-year average was **7.32%** on Oct. 22, 2026, up from 6.19% a year earlier ([Freddie Mac PMMS](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)). For many Charleston homeowners, a rate-only refinance won't save money right now. It can still make sense to:

- **Remove FHA mortgage insurance** by moving to conventional once you have enough equity.
- **Take cash out** for renovations, a raised foundation, or to consolidate higher-interest debt.
- **Move from an adjustable rate** to a fixed rate.
- **Consider a HELOC** (home equity line of credit) to tap equity while keeping a low first-mortgage rate.

**Break-even point (definition):** closing costs divided by monthly savings. Ask us for a free rate check and we'll show you whether refinancing, a HELOC, or staying put makes the most sense.
